# Art Direction content audit and improvement plan

## Scope and diagnosis

- Target: `public/handbook/interactive-art-direction-handbook.html` and its generated app module.
- Before revision: six sections; the only substantive section was a generic learning-layer table.
- Art direction was reduced to brand adjectives, motion tokens and hero variations. It did not teach brief interpretation, concept development, reference analysis, narrative, visual hierarchy, production leadership, critique or case-study presentation.
- The document mentioned Awwwards-style work only as an effect-copying failure and offered no agency-grade process for producing or presenting an original project.

## Quality bar

The page must teach how an art director turns strategy into a creative thesis, directs every sensory and interactive layer, aligns a multidisciplinary production team, edits the result under technical constraints and presents the reasoning honestly in an award-caliber case study.

## Implemented structure

1. Define the art director's role and the brief-to-thesis process.
2. Teach reference deconstruction, narrative sequencing and frame hierarchy.
3. Direct typography, imagery, motion, interaction, technology and sound as one language.
4. Concentrate effort into signature moments while systemizing repeatable rules and responsive adaptations.
5. Provide agency deliverables, production stages, critique language and multidisciplinary decision points.
6. Add award-submission review lenses and a complete material-brand campaign example.
7. Protect concept, artifact, review-lens and case-study depth with deterministic tests.
